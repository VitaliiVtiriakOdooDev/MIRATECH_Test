# Assumptions

- Odoo version is unspecified in the task; the demonstration uses Odoo Community 17.

- Reporting year and demo year: 2026; chosen period is a calendar month.
- All three companies are wholly owned; USD is the group currency.
- Fixed demo rates: 1 USD = 40 UAH = 4 PLN; all report lines use the closing rate.
- Ukraine invoices USD 50,000 and Poland USD 30,000 monthly; all work is consumed as incurred.
- Poland's February invoice is credited by USD 5,000 in February.
- Poland receives the USD 20,000 payment on 1 April; the group's March clearing shows cash in transit.
- Each company has USD 100,000 equivalent external sales and USD 40,000 equivalent external costs monthly, including payroll.
- The loan is outstanding and interest-free; both sides record it on 15 January.
- Journals are tax-free; there are no minority interests or inventory profit.
- Intercompany transactions use the relevant company commercial partner and non-trade accounts are explicitly marked.
- Earlier P&L is classified as retained earnings for the balance sheet.
